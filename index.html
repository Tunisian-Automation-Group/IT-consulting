FAQ

Questions European buyers ask us first

Who do we sign the contract with?

With our German GmbH, under German law, with jurisdiction in Germany. Every consultant and engineer on your project is bound by the same confidentiality and data protection obligations.

How do you handle GDPR and data residency?

Personal data stays in EU cloud regions you choose. Engineers access it through your identity provider with role-based permissions. We sign a data processing agreement and name a contact for data protection questions.

Who owns the code and intellectual property?

You do. All work product, including documentation and infrastructure code, transfers to you on payment of each invoice. We do not reuse client code.

How fast can a team start?

Discovery starts within a week of the first call. A pilot squad is typically working on your backlog in week three or four. Larger scale-ups are planned quarterly from our existing bench plus targeted hiring.

Which languages do the teams work in?

German and English as standard. French is available for Francophone clients and subsidiaries. Documentation is written in the language you choose.

Will the team work on site with us?

Yes. Our consultants are with you for kick-offs, workshops and key milestones, and remote collaboration covers the day-to-day. You are also welcome at our office in Germany at any time.
